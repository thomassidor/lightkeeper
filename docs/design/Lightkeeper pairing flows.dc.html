Lightkeeper pairing

Five devices, one paradigm

Each device is a row: the happy flow left to right, then the special cases to the right of the divider. The paradigm is the one settled in the iteration canvas (turn 4g, summarised in README.md) — intro, one control per screen, round handles on a live day strip, times folded into the group they belong to, everything else deferred to the device's settings, which Homey owns. Circadian's day screen is live; drag it.

The rules Intro names the decisions One control per screen Three steps, dotted Defaults pre-filled Times inside their group Caveats wait until after pairing One brightness control everywhere Homey asks the name, so pairing never does

→ Bar height is brightness and bar colour is the colour, and the Off-to-Full axis on the left says so. With “Set brightness too” switched off — the default — the bars are flat and the chart is purely a day of colour.
→ Every point is an absolute clock time. This device is for “cool at 09:00, ember at 22:30”, which is a routine, not a sun position — sun-relative belongs to circadian.
