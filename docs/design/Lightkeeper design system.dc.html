Design system

Five type sizes, four radii, three border roles, two button shapes. Everything here is in use in the flows file — where a value was ambiguous it has been collapsed to one, and the rule for choosing is stated. Anything not on this page is not in the system.

Rules
One family, no webfont and no display face:
-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, ‘Segoe UI’, Roboto, ‘Helvetica Neue’, sans-serif
The old half-pixel sizes — 12.5, 13.5, 14.5 — are gone. Nothing distinguished them at a glance and every new screen had to guess between them.
16 is the control size: group headings and every button share it, which is why it sits so close to body 15.
Two weights do real work — 500 for row titles, 600 for headings and controls. 700 is only for screen titles.
Never write the font shorthand with inherit as the family. It is invalid, the whole declaration is dropped, and the text silently falls back to 16px/400 — this was live in 89 places. Use longhands.
Negative tracking only at 20px and above; positive tracking only on the 11px eyebrow. Row metadata sits 2px below its title, never beside it.

Spacing & metrics
Phone screen width 390px. Screen body padding 18px 16px 24px; top and bottom chrome 13–15px.
Every tappable row is at least 44px tall with border-box sizing. Every button is 48px.
Row padding is 12px 0 inside a 0 14px gutter, so the divider runs to the text edge rather than the card edge.
Gaps: 6 between pips, 10–12 between a control and its label, 14 between stacked blocks, 20–22 between screens on the canvas.
Cards stacked outside a group sit 7px apart; grouped cards sit 10px apart.

Voice

Plain, British, second person, no exclamation marks
Rules
Titles are questions or plain statements of outcome — “Which lights?”, “Warm at night, cool at noon”. Sentence case throughout; no title case anywhere.
British spelling: colour, not color.
Subtitles explain the benefit, not the mechanism: “Your lights follow the sun instead of sitting at one colour all day.”
Errors name the fact first, then what follows from it. No apology, no “oops”, no emoji.
In-flight labels are the present participle of the button’s own verb — Save becomes Saving…, Test becomes Testing… — so the label never jumps to an unrelated word.
Numbers are concrete and unit-suffixed — 41 lx, 2700 K, 2 of 6, Step 1 of 3 — and times are 24-hour, as in 21:00.
Iconography
←→›×✓
There is no icon set. The interface uses five text glyphs at the ambient font size, and a chevron is rotated 90° to open rather than swapped for another glyph.
The serif italic “i” badge that used to mark hints has been removed — it was the only serif in the product and the only drawn icon.
Colour, shape and the 24-hour ramp carry meaning instead of icons. If something needs an icon to be understood, the label is wrong.
No emoji, ever.
