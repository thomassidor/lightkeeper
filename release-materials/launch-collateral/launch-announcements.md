# Launch announcements

## Website

### One remote. Several lights. Zero repetitive setup.

Light Link is a community app for Homey Pro that turns the remotes, switches and dials already in your home into flexible light controllers. Pick a source, select lights or a zone, assign each press or turn, and Light Link creates and maintains the Flows underneath.

It began with three IKEA BILRESA scroll-wheel remotes and a choice: manually create 27 Flows, or spend a couple of days making the repetition disappear for good. The result works across compatible already-paired controls and lights, stays local, has no telemetry and is open source.

Requires Homey Pro 2023 or later and a Personal API Key with Flow permissions. Compatibility depends on which events the source's owning integration exposes.

## Customer email

Subject: Meet Light Link - one setup for all those remote-to-light Flows

I bought three scroll-wheel remotes and realised that connecting each one to three lights for three actions meant building 27 Flows by hand. I could have done that in under an hour. Instead, I made Light Link.

Choose a paired remote, pick lights or a zone, assign each press or turn, and save. Light Link builds and maintains the Flows for you, so changing a room later does not mean rebuilding everything. It runs locally on Homey Pro, has no telemetry and keeps its required API key on your Homey.
