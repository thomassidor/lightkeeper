# Brand authorization position

Light Link is an original independent community app and does not claim to represent Homey, IKEA, Philips Hue or another device manufacturer. Its name, icons and imagery are original. No third-party brand logo, official product render or Homey product image is used.

Manufacturer and model names appear only in fixture, compatibility and review documentation to identify hardware against which behaviour is tested. The store listing should not be presented as an official manufacturer integration.

Accordingly, there is no third-party commissioning or brand-authorization letter for the Light Link identity. [OWNER ACTION] If the publisher later markets the app as official, uses manufacturer artwork, or adds a manufacturer brand to the app name, obtain and retain written authorization before submission.
