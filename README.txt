EARLY ACCESS: Lightkeeper is a 0.x app in early development. An update can change or break a device you have already set up, which may then need Repair or adding again. Please read the changelog before updating.

Lightkeeper does four things with lights you have already paired:
A Light Remote turns any remote, switch or dial into a controller for them.
A light schedule switches them on and off at the times and on the days you choose.
A circadian light or a Colour Curve Light moves them through the colours of the day, following your own sunrise and sunset or a curve you draw.
A Room-sensing Light sets their brightness from a light sensor you own, or from how high the sun is.

Light Remotes and schedules write and maintain the Homey Flows for you, so they need a Personal API Key. The other three need no key. Every device's values are Flow tags, and two Flow cards set a room from your Lightkeeper devices or ask whether it is dark enough.

Everything runs on your Homey: no cloud, no telemetry. Designed and written with AI, verified on real hardware.
