This app exists because I bought three scroll wheel remotes from IKEA. Three remotes, three lights each, three things I wanted each remote to do — which, once I opened the Flow editor, added up to 27 Flows to build by hand. Half an hour of clicking, maybe an hour. I couldn't be bothered. So I spent a couple of days building an app instead. Or rather, I had AI build one.

Here is what it does. Pick a remote, switch or dial you have already paired. Pick the lights you want it to control, individually or a whole zone at once. Then say what each press, hold or turn should do: on, off, brighter, dimmer, warmer, colder. Every row has a Test button that drives the real lights immediately, so you know it works before you save. Light Link writes the Flows underneath and keeps them maintained as things change — and if you move a lamp into a room a controller points at, it follows. You never open the Flow editor.

What it needs: a Homey Pro 2023 or later, and a Personal API Key with Flow permissions. That second one is unusual, so it is worth saying why — Homey does not let an app create Flows on its own, and generating Flows is the entire trick here. The key stays on your Homey, is used only for writing Flows, and is never logged or included in a diagnostics report. If it ever stops working your remotes keep controlling your lights; only Flow maintenance pauses until you paste in a new one, and you lose nothing you set up.

No telemetry, no cloud, nothing leaves your Homey. Designed and written with AI, directed and verified on real hardware by me. The full source is public, so you can check all of that rather than take my word for it.
